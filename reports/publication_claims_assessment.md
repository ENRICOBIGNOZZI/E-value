The scientific case rests most strongly on the explicit moment frontier and
the learned-reference information/utility calculations. Validity of a Catoni

## Material limitations that remain in the scientific interpretation

The sharp waiting results exclude changing regimes, transaction costs,
growing dimension, a jointly vanishing ridge and competing episode closures.
Their adaptive null class matters. The finite scalar diagnostic has few
crossings, so it cannot establish a precise ranking from the asymptotic
coefficient. The paper states these limits.

The controlled deadline policies have different actual null rejection rates.
A common nominal cap does not equalize those rates. Working-model power,
the certificate optimizer and asymptotic log growth are different objectives.
The fund's stakes are not changed using the controlled study or pilot outcomes.

The financial search retains individual guarantees and lacks a search-wide
5% guarantee. Its event labels are evaluator quantities, not the datewise
conditional null. Positive next-period gains do not certify lifetime trade
value; absence of PARK/REVIVE in the pilot does not demonstrate adaptation.
All Signals is already effectively sparse and the model charges no fixed
per-signal research cost. These facts constrain the economic conclusion;
they are not reasons to alter the declared simulation after observing it.
