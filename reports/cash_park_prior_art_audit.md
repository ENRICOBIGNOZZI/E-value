## Mapping the manuscript's claims

Theorem 17 fixes the return experiment, training scheme and estimated reference.
Its score mean and variance both depend on the same shrinking portfolio target.
The proof constructs an admissible adaptive null for that instance. The
information scale is log n, and the attained coefficient for cumulative
conditional and realized utility is 4/gamma per log(1/alpha). The proof gives
pathwise limits and a lower bound in probability for valid test families; it
does not claim expected stopped-time or stopped-utility equivalence. The
likelihood, quadratic strong-law and martingale-normalization steps specialize
standard tools. An exhaustive check for this precise economic example remains
outstanding.

Proposition 18 fixes the same return experiment and reference learner, with a
constant Minkowski score certificate. Under its alternative, the conditional
factor expectation is bounded by exp(lambda_t delta_t - d lambda_t²/2), where
d is a strictly positive deterministic gap. Completing the square gives a
common compensator independent of the betting rule. Its expected total is
finite because the reference's fourth powers are summable in expectation.
Compensation, Ville and Markov yield a bound uniform over predictable policies,
including level-dependent policies. This calculation does not assert a new
maximal inequality or an impossibility for every valid test. The economic
lower-bound linear rule still has eventual power one in that same instance.

Allowing the certificate to shrink with the observable target changes the
family covered by Proposition 18. The geometry-dependent Catoni rule already
has the same logarithmic exponent as the linear rule in Theorem 17 under its
specified moment contract. A financial learner, allocation state, trading
costs, minimum-effect floor and annual competing closures require a separate
analysis. No scalar conclusion is silently transferred to the full fund.

The constant-effect deadline study has no finite compensator budget of this
form. Its retained results therefore remain compatible with the new ceiling.
Its Bellman objective and direct statistical antecedents are reviewed separately
in `horizon_prior_art_audit.md`; neither study establishes a general best
testing method or a financial-performance gain.

## Outcome of this audit

The subsequent Proposition 19 also specializes the same summable-remainder
and martingale-normalization argument to bounded predictable geometric bets.
It computes the price of a prescribed oversized second-moment certificate
and an attaining past-only raw-moment stake. The claim is the instance-specific
rate and waiting coefficient, not a new general adaptive-variance principle,
deadline-power optimizer or information bound for every certificate using the
public moment information.

The manuscript's attribution is more specific, and one previously omitted
nearby lower-bound source is now cited. The inspected statements do not by
themselves establish the full estimated-policy/cumulative-utility result, but
that observation is not evidence of priority. Broad novelty assessment and the
complete final financial experiment remain open. No parameter, seed, endpoint,
comparator or economic acceptance criterion was changed after this review.
