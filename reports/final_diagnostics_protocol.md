# Supplementary final-study graphics

This reporting protocol is fixed on 17 October 2026, while the original
100-market pilot is still running and before any final market is generated.
It completes descriptive graphics requested in the supplied handoff. It does
not change the frozen scientific configuration, primary contrast, sample size,
inference, methods, thresholds or economic tolerances. Earlier pilot results
have been inspected. It is not a pre-pilot registration.

The final representative market is fixed at **60001**, the first declared
final seed, before observing final output. The previously fixed pilot example
remains 50001. Neither is selected for performance or regime transitions.

The production command accepts only the complete 500-market final pool
60001–60500 through the existing final-manuscript evidence gate: current
scientific/analysis freeze, sealed reports, current numeric and independent
event-reconstruction audits, exact frozen assessment and byte-current raw
replay. Detailed economic events must be sealed too. It reads actual runtime
files and the fixed representative daily ledger from that same raw pool.
All evidence is revalidated after plotting. A new directory is published
atomically; existing output, evidence directories and the manuscript cannot
be overwritten. The live simulation/controller and core packet builder are
unchanged.

Eight pages are retained regardless of outcomes:

1. Actual net wealth and permitted support in the fixed representative market,
   with all six methods and initial wealth one. Waiting dates are included.
2. Empirical CDFs of conventional and HAC Sharpe ratios, with defined market
   counts in every legend. Undefined ratios are omitted and never imputed.
3. Empirical CDFs of the paired scaled-utility differences for Tail minus
   every other method. Every market, comparator and negative value is retained.
   This adds no test or confidence interval to the frozen analysis.
4. Scaled mean utility, cumulative proportional cost, cumulative quadratic
   cost and target turnover distributions for all methods.
5. Executed deployment counts by positive, near-zero and negative next-period
   actual-state conditional gain, with the common ±1e-8 daily-utility
   tolerance, and distributions of all signed gains. Terminal choices are
   excluded. Empty groups remain explicit. These are economic errors, not
   type-I errors or lifetime trade values.
6. The representative search methods' annual signal-support states. Never
   selected, selected, and previously selected but inactive are distinct.
   The last category can follow a PARK or a swap removal; it is not labeled
   as a count of PARK decisions. Only states with actual OOS returns appear.
7. Stable-event responses within 1/3/5 years among cohorts with that full
   calendar follow-up. Tables retain every denominator. Three-year outcomes
   distinguish selected, competing move, sign reversal, shortlist withdrawal,
   administrative end and still unselected. These are endogenous method-specific
   populations; dependent event counts do not become independent markets.
   Competing closures are not treated as independent censoring, and
   success-only mean delays or Kaplan–Meier estimates are not substituted.
8. Measured annual-block computation times and per-market sums, all methods
   combined. This excludes independent arithmetic replay and orchestration;
   it is not the parallel study's wall time or a portable speed comparison.

The existing core packet supplies cross-market wealth bands, support counts
and evidence paths. The controlled stationary/deadline sections supply power
curves. The supplementary pages complete the other graphics without adding
simulations or changing the reported financial sample.

Every plotted summary also has a CSV table. Verification records input,
output, source, evidence-gate and protocol hashes. A generated PDF still needs
rendered visual inspection before publication; successful code execution is
not a claim of visual QA or overall paper completion.

Pure preparation/rendering functions may be exercised on explicitly labeled
software fixtures or an already sealed pilot preview. Such a preview is
outside the final production command, retains its actual sample size in every
page title and cannot pass as a final packet. It adds no independent markets.
